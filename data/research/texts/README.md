# Preserved text research

Original downloaded Markdown, PDFs and images from `resources/texts/` are preserved here byte-for-byte. The [migration manifest](../../../spec/archive/legacy-research/migration-manifest.json) records their original names, sizes and hashes. These files are historical source evidence, not a claim that a current URL was successfully fetched or completely extracted.

Active source lists are [`.urls.md.conf`](../../../.urls.md.conf) and [`.urls.pdf.conf`](../../../.urls.pdf.conf) at the project root. Repeatable imports and validation reports live in [spec/analyses/texts](../../../spec/analyses/texts/), with private immutable versions and exports in the configured text cache. Image links are preserved as originally downloaded, including the `.images` directory.
