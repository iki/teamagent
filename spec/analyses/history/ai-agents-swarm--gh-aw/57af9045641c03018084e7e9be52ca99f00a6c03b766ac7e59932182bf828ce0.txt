The complete [admission compiler](https://github.com/github/gh-aw/blob/3e66bf448eb769907aa571fa35664821a691b965/pkg/workflow/compiler_admission.go) creates a bounded admission job with content-read and OIDC permissions, compiler-owned setup, and a SHA-256 identity derived from frontmatter and body. It separates conclusion/notification work from the agent job and preserves direct dependency conditions where explicit status functions would change GitHub's implicit success behavior. The generated condition accepts admission success or a skipped admission job. **Admission is optional/configured, not an unconditional external approval gate**; the admission service runtime and outage policy were not reviewed. Selected [compiler fixtures](https://github.com/github/gh-aw/blob/3e66bf448eb769907aa571fa35664821a691b965/pkg/workflow/compiler_admission_test.go#L439-L502) pin dependency-condition behavior.

The complete [step-token validator](https://github.com/github/gh-aw/blob/3e66bf448eb769907aa571fa35664821a691b965/pkg/workflow/safe_outputs_step_token_validation.go) follows configured global, mention and per-output references. It checks that a token producer is in the consuming job and precedes the consumer; conclusion work cannot rely on a user step's token mint. Its [fixtures](https://github.com/github/gh-aw/blob/3e66bf448eb769907aa571fa35664821a691b965/pkg/workflow/safe_outputs_step_token_validation_test.go) cover missing, misplaced and out-of-order producers. This is a rendered-workflow reference check, not a universal proof of credential confidentiality or runtime authorization.

The inspected [safe-output manifest](https://github.com/github/gh-aw/blob/3e66bf448eb769907aa571fa35664821a691b965/actions/setup/js/safe_output_manifest.cjs#L1-L347) binds a recorder to a claim identity and recursively redacts configured secrets in keys and strings before JSON serialization. This matters because escaping during serialization can obscure a secret's original representation. Claim-backed redaction failure throws; the non-claim path has a minimal fallback. Filesystem permission helpers and every logging path were not audited.

Receipt extraction distinguishes final resource creation from prepared custom actions, staged/skipped/deferred/cancelled/delegated results and buffered pull-request reviews. Explicit success can produce a success status; legacy entries without a success field can still produce records without that status. A URL by itself therefore does not mean an action completed. Selected [redaction fixtures](https://github.com/github/gh-aw/blob/3e66bf448eb769907aa571fa35664821a691b965/actions/setup/js/safe_output_manifest.test.cjs#L227-L284) and [action-state fixtures](https://github.com/github/gh-aw/blob/3e66bf448eb769907aa571fa35664821a691b965/actions/setup/js/safe_output_manifest.test.cjs#L436-L529) cover escaped secrets, serialization/write failure and staged versus final action records.

**Harness consequence:** Freeze the compiled workflow, compiler version and effective configuration as one execution identity. Keep planner output separate from authoritative admission, and give credentials explicit job and time scopes. Record intent, staging, accepted work, application and publication separately, preserving provider-native identifiers without inferring completion from a link. Redact structured content before encoding, while retaining bounded, non-secret evidence of failures. None of this makes optional admission mandatory or establishes the cloud runtime's isolation.
