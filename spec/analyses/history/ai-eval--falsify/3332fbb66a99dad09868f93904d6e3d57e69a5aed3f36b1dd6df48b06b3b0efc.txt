It adds structured JSON outcomes for lock/hash/verify, distinguishing hash-only OK with observed null from metric PASS, and separating missing lock from dataset mismatch even when exit codes coincide. Dataset verification hashes supplied bytes in chunks; without the optional dataset argument, the declared dataset commitment is not independently checked.

The complete [observed-value fixtures](https://github.com/studio-11-co/falsify/blob/4b6aece648f9db1b8cd586b2e982942982389344/tests/test_prml_verify_observed.py) cover finite pass/fail, infinity/NaN and malformed numbers. These are useful evolution-gate contracts: a matching manifest hash proves commitment consistency, not correctness of a caller-supplied measurement, and predicate PASS does not establish independently reproduced performance.
