## Category assessment

Document conversion, repository packaging, content detection, hybrid search, and bounded model-guided retrieval decisions.

Compose specialist importers with evidence-preserving retrieval. Docling and MarkItDown normalize documents; Repomix packages repositories; Trafilatura extracts pages; QMD supplies a hybrid-search baseline. Jevgrep and Jev Ultrafast illustrate bounded decisions that complement deterministic candidate filtering rather than replacing provenance and execution policy.

Parser success does not imply complete extraction, graph/semantic retrieval is not always cost-effective, and ANN performance depends on workload. Keep omission accounting and source spans; evaluate lexical baselines before adding expensive reranking or model-guided traversal.
