## Category assessment

Provider normalization, deployment routing, authentication compatibility, and command-output reduction.

Shortlist Bifrost and LiteLLM as replaceable gateways after protocol and workload tests. Keep task-level quality routing outside deployment balancing. RTK contributes recoverable compression rather than inference routing. Treat CLIProxyAPI as a separately configured compatibility route whose upstream and entitlement semantics require explicit verification.

An API-shaped interface does not guarantee identical tools, reasoning, media, or subscription rights. Performance claims were not reproduced; a working proxy is neither a durable agent runtime nor a sandbox.
